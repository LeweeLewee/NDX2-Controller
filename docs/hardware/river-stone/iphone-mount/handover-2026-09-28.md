## 2. iPhone 11 CAD reference

- **Source:** [GrabCAD "iPhone 11" by Leonid](https://grabcad.com/library/iphone-11-6), SolidWorks 2020, STEP AP214 in mm. It is a community model, not Apple-certified, and GrabCAD site terms apply.
- **File:** 28,909,433 bytes, SHA-256 `ab204f6316407f9c7d9d6a7ec7a97fdd5a2abde12e56e619c3cbceff6474a561`. It is one valid solid with 114 faces. It sits in the user's Downloads folder; add it under ignored `local/`, or record the URL and hash only.
- **Body:** the widest section, 0.2 mm behind mid-thickness, measures **150.91 × 75.72 × 8.32 mm**. Apple's nominal is 150.9 × 75.7 × 8.3 ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/111865)).
  - The sides are rounded: about 1.8 mm narrower per side at 3.5 mm from mid-thickness.
  - The plan-view corners are a B-spline blend, about 14 × 14.7 mm, not a single radius.
- **Features (CAD-derived):**
  - camera plateau 25.06 × 28.26 mm, R6.985, 0.70 mm proud; the lens rings are not modelled;
  - Lightning opening 8.54 × 2.80 mm, centred across the width;
  - two groups of six 1.45 mm holes on the bottom edge, which one is the microphone is not identified;
  - button spans, measured from the top end: ring/silent 20.6–26.2 mm, volume 30.6–54.7 mm, side button 34.1–51.2 mm; all 0.37–0.42 mm proud.
- **Not modelled:** the display active area and the notch outline. Full values are in `iphone11-reference-check.json`.

## 3. Aperture corner correction (R3 to R6.5)

![Printed R3 corner exposing inactive area](v3-print-corner-photo.jpg)

![Derived correction](aperture-corner-correction.png)

**Derivation.** Assumptions: display centred on the phone body, circular corner approximation.

- **Active area:** 1792 × 828 px at 326 ppi ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/111865)), which gives **139.62 × 64.51 mm**.
- **Display corner radius:** **41.5 pt**, community-reported for the iPhone XR/11 ([device_corner_radius](https://github.com/rehmatsg/device_corner_radius/blob/main/README.md), [View-Finder PR 63](https://github.com/S1xinch/View-Finder/pull/63)). At 2 px/pt that is 83 px, or **6.47 mm**. This is not an Apple drawing value.
- **Edge insets:** the accepted aperture sits 0.36 mm inside the active area at the ends and 0.41 mm along the long edges. This matches the user's acceptance of the straight edges.

**Candidate radii** (from `corner-analysis.json`):

| Aperture radius | Corner margin inside active area (centred) | Exposed inactive area per corner | Worst margin with 0.2 mm display offset |
|---|---|---|---|
| R3 (printed) | −0.89 mm | 3.36 mm² | −1.18 mm |
| R5 | −0.07 mm | 0.15 mm² | −0.35 mm |
| R5.5 | +0.14 mm | 0 | −0.14 mm |
| R6.0 | +0.35 mm | 0 | +0.07 mm |
| **R6.5 (adopt)** | **+0.36 mm** | **0** | **+0.16 mm** |

The model's −0.89 mm prediction for R3 matches the sliver visible at every printed corner.

**Why R6.5:** it is the smallest radius at which the corners keep the same margin as the straight edges (0.36 mm). It still stays inside the active area with 0.2 mm of registration error.

**Residual risk:** the display corner is a continuous curve, not a true circle, and the display's position on the body isn't measured. If a sliver remains at one end only, the display is offset. Correct that by moving the aperture, not by changing the radius again.

## 4. Fit sample v3 (physically passed)

- **Package:** in `iPhone fit sample v3/` in the user's Downloads folder: 0.15, 0.20 and 0.25 mm variants as STL and STEP, plus `validation.json`, `README.md`, the generator `iphone_mount_sample_v3.py` and `outline_pts.py`.
- **Snug wall:** it follows the convex outline of the real phone at its widest section, with the relief zones excluded. Parameters:
  - wall 2.4 mm;
  - lip 1.2 mm;
  - wall top at z = 8.0 mm;
  - lead-in 0.8 mm high with 0.5 mm extra clearance.
- **Reliefs:**
  - wall removed over each button span plus 3 mm at each end;
  - Lightning and grille end open over 52.8 mm;
  - top end open over 36 mm;
  - rear fully open.
- **CAD and mesh checks** (both models tessellated at 0.01 mm):
  - the phone and frame don't overlap at 0.4 or 0.8 mm spacers;
  - snug-zone clearance equals the variant (0.149 / 0.199 / 0.249 mm);
  - buttons 3.0 mm, Lightning and grille features 2.44 mm, camera 6.5 mm or more.
- **Outer size:** 155.98 × 80.96 × 8.0 mm, against 170 × 95 mm for v2. The surround beyond the phone outline is 2.6 mm.
- **Status:** physical fit passed. Retention, insertion force and acoustics are not yet tested.
