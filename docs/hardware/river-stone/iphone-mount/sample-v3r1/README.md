## Print and fit procedure — 24 October 2026

1. Print **one snug-frame-0p20-r6p5.stl** linked above. This has the side-locating walls and corrected corners. Do not use the full shell or the historical R3 frame for this trial. Also print four copies each of [0.4 mm spacers](../sample-v2/spacer-0.4mm.stl) and [0.8 mm spacers](../sample-v2/spacer-0.8mm.stl). Keep the two sets separate; each strip is 12 × 3 mm.
2. P1S / 0.4 mm nozzle: use familiar plain PLA and the matching filament/plate profile, 0.20 mm layers including the first layer, three walls, 15% infill, supports off, 100% scale. Flat front face on the bed, locating walls upwards, as exported. Frame size should be approximately **156 × 81 × 8 mm**. Keep spacers flat: 0.4 mm gives two layers, 0.8 mm four. Inspect the slicer preview for all eight strips. These are starting settings, not a validated sliced plate or time estimate.
3. Let the parts cool. Remove strings and sharp edges, especially from surfaces that could touch glass. Clean the phone and sample. Remove the phone case; leave any fitted screen protector in place and report it with the result. Avoid sanding the locating walls before the first fit check, since that changes the fit being tested.
4. Put four 0.4 mm strips on the inner lip, evenly distributed, two along each long side, entirely under the phone's inactive perimeter. Keep them clear of lit pixels, curved display corners, sensors and microphone openings. Use a bright screen to establish placement. If all four cannot sit safely and flat, omit the spacer comparison and report that rather than forcing contact. Do not stack or mix thicknesses.
5. Load the phone **from the open rear**, screen facing the aperture. Align the wider short-end opening with the Lightning connector and the narrower one with the top/notch; check the side reliefs align with the buttons. Hold both parts throughout: the sample has no rear retention. The phone should settle with gentle finger pressure, without squeezing the frame, pressing buttons or forcing it into place. Lift it out from behind.
6. With a bright full-screen image, inspect all four corners and edges, straight on and at a normal seated viewing angle. Check for exposed inactive black slivers, visibly uneven masking and excess coverage of useful screen content. Test taps and swipes near all four edges in landscape. Compare four 0.8 mm strips if desired: nominal glass setback changes from 1.6 to 2.0 mm including the 1.2 mm lip. Choose whichever provides comfortable access and concealment; this is a preference check, not a requirement to tighten the fit.
7. Plug in the measured lead. Check that it seats fully without pressing the frame or covering the adjacent grille openings. Check buttons are free and the rear camera is untouched. Make the same short voice recording bare and in the hand-supported sample, using the same app, position and distance; listen for an obvious change. This open sample cannot establish final closed-stone microphone performance.

Report: **fit** (tight / comfortable / loose), **four corners** (concealed / exposed, which), **spacer preference** (0.4 / 0.8 / comparison skipped), **edge touch** (easy / obstructed), **cable and buttons** (clear / interference), **voice** (similar / worse / not tested), and whether a screen protector is fitted. A front-on photo with a bright screen and one side view are useful, but no precision measurements are needed unless a particular fault appears.
